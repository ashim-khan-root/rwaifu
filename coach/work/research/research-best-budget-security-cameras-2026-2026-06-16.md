### 4. Feature Deep‑Dive  

#### 4.1 Video Quality & Compression  
* **H.265/HEVC** is now standard even on $30‑$50 cams (Wyze v4, Reolink 3 Pro). This reduces bandwidth by ~50 % vs. H.264, cutting cloud/storage fees.  
* **1080p** remains the sweet spot for budget; 4K cameras still start at $120‑$150.

#### 4.2 Night Vision  
* **Dual‑LED IR** (Wyze, Eufy) gives clearer monochrome images than single‑LED units.  
* **Color night‑vision** (Reolink 3 Pro) uses low‑intensity RGB LEDs—great for wildlife monitoring but adds $5‑$10 to price.
